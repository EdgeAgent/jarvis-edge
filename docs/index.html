Questions

Before you ask.

Does anything leave my computer?

No. The models, your documents, the chat history and the brain map all live on your machine; the console talks to Ollama on localhost. Later roadmap stages add optional online workers — calendar, email — behind the same approval gate, and only if you connect them.

What computer do I need?

Any current Windows, Mac, or Linux machine. The 8B brain is comfortable on 8–16 GB of RAM; a GPU and more memory let you run larger models. The console itself is light.

Can it send email or post for me?

Yes — when you say so. Jarvis drafts the work and parks it in the approval queue with the full text in front of you. Nothing fires on its own; the moment you approve an item, it executes — through channels you connect, like your own automation endpoint (n8n, Make, your mail stack). Approval isn’t a formality. It’s the trigger.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

Different thing. That's a rented chatbot in someone else's building. This is a crew and a command center on your hardware, reading your files, that you pay for once. If you only need a chatbot, you don't need this.

What does the 95% roadmap mean?

Jarvis is being built to take on 95% of daily workload — desktop work, online work, life admin — under one rule: it takes your direction, then finds the best, fastest, most secure route itself. The last 5% stays human: final sends, payments, credentials, judgment calls. The roadmap is in the repository, and it's included in the $8.99.
