Straight answers

Before you ask

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch and no tier wall. Jarvis-Edge is MIT-licensed open source, and the models it runs (via Ollama) are free open-weight models. Your costs are your electricity. There is no account, no subscription, and nothing to cancel.

Does my data go to the cloud?

No. Models, documents, chat history and the brain map all live on your machine. The console talks to Ollama on localhost. Optional online workers (calendar, email) arrive in later roadmap stages — behind the same approval gate, and only if you wire them.

What computer do I need?

Any modern Windows, macOS or Linux machine. The 8B brain runs comfortably on 8–16 GB of RAM; bigger models reward a GPU and more memory. The console itself is light — the model does the heavy lifting.

Can it send email or post for me?

It drafts, you decide. Everything outbound parks in an approval queue with the full text visible. Jarvis has no send button of his own — that is a deliberate, permanent design rule.

How is this different from ChatGPT / Copilot?

Those are rented brains in someone else's building, billed monthly, reading what you paste. Jarvis-Edge is a crew and a command center on your hardware, reading your files, working while the subscription services are still asking you to log in.
